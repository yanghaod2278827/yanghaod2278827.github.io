I'm Hao Yang, a PhD student at the Institute of Manufacturing Engineering, Huaqiao University. My research is **brazed diamond wire saws**: brazing diamond grit onto a thin steel wire, then using it to slice hard, brittle semiconductor materials like sapphire and silicon nitride. Most days I'm either in the lab or on an SEM; the rest of the time I write Python to hand off the repetitive, error-prone work.

## What I'm working on

- Brazing, wire preparation, and cutting performance of diamond wire saws — the core of my dissertation
- Small tools for Claude Code: [claude-statusline](https://github.com/yanghaod2278827/claude-statusline) (counting what I actually spend on third-party APIs) and [claude-worklog-dida](https://github.com/yanghaod2278827/claude-worklog-dida) (turning what I did each day into completed tasks automatically)

## This site

Built with Hugo, hosted on GitHub Pages. Two kinds of writing: things that bit me — the ones I looked up once and will look up again — and notes on whether some repetitive task can be handed to a program. The star count on the homepage is fetched from GitHub by a script every night; the article count is computed by Hugo. Neither is typed in by hand.
